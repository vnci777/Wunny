What Are Tones?

A tone is how your voice moves on a word. It can stay high, stay low, rise, or fall.

In Cantonese, tone changes a word's meaning. The same sound can mean six different things!

Think of your voice like a ladder. It can stay still, climb up, or slide down.

How to Tell Tones Apart

1

Does it move?

  • Flat tones stay still: 136
  • Rising tones go up: 25
  • Falling tone goes down: 4
2

Flat? Listen for height

  • 1 is high.
  • 3 is middle.
  • 6 is low.
3

Rising? Listen for size

  • 2 is a big climb.
  • 5 is a small climb.
  • 4 is the only one that drops!

Tricky Pairs

Wait, Isn't It Nine Tones?

Technically, yes! Traditional Cantonese counts 9 tones. The extra three are short versions of tones you already know.

6long tones
+
3short tones
=
9tones in all
Why does Wunny only teach 6? The 3 short tones only happen in words ending in -p, -t or -k. Your voice stops quickly, but the pitch is the same as Tone 1, 3 or 6. So Jyutping, and Wunny, just write them as 1, 3 and 6. Learn the six, and you already know all nine!
